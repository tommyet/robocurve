# Measuring the painted result from a photograph

This revision replaces cumulative emitted-paint fraction with **visible painted surface area**. The task represents repainting a small household decorative plaque while protecting its surroundings. Both plaque coverage and paint on the surrounding backing use the same visible-blue criterion.

Take a square-on photograph before and after spraying, with the entire 360×360mm backing in frame and a ruler for scale. Use consistent diffuse lighting and exposure. Rectify the image to the backing square. For each surface, retain its clean reference color and a fully blue reference swatch photographed under the same lighting. In linear RGB, project the observed color change along the clean-to-blue reference vector; a blue blend fraction of at least0.5 counts as painted. Apply exactly this criterion on the white plaque and grey backing. A simple image editor can threshold the calibrated image and count pixels using the ruler scale. The swatches are measurement references, not extra robot targets.

Coverage is painted plaque area divided by plaque area. Off-plaque paint is painted backing area outside the plaque, in cm². The fixed80mm-diameter plaque has area50.2655cm². The off-plaque allowance is15% of that fixed area: **7.5398cm²**, irrespective of how much paint was emitted. At least50% coverage earns the coating half-score; at least90% earns the three-quarter milestone. All prior cap-handling and final physical hold requirements also apply. Repeatedly spraying an already blue patch cannot dilute the off-plaque measurement.

The simulation uses10mm square surface cells with a uniform tint per cell: alpha=min(1,dose/0.08s). Thus the photograph rule alpha≥0.5 corresponds to dose≥0.04s; this is an appearance model, not a claim of calibrated aerosol opacity. Each painted cell contributes its actual circle-intersection area to plaque coverage and its complementary area to off-plaque paint. Boundary cells are not assigned wholesale by their centres. The visual circle uses128segments with maximum radial discrepancy below13µm; adjacent tiles meet without deliberate gaps. The deposition quadrature is coarse and uncalibrated.

Only the specified backing plane is a deposition surface in this prototype. Spraying outside the supported cone/backing/range geometry invalidates the episode rather than silently treating unobserved paint as clean. This is a separate, reproducible geometric validity check; emitted mass is not the photo score. See scoring.py for the exact supported-domain predicate.

A full-score hold requires consecutive qualifying measured states spanning at least1.0s. The runner then retains21 consecutive full-score states at20Hz before terminating, so the video can show a further full second at score1.0. Neither a repeated video frame nor a frozen still counts as native hold evidence.
