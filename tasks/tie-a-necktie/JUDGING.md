# Judging the necktie

Use front and rear photographs, the tying video and a ruler. The collar form
has a marked centre. Measure tip length downward from the neck form's
lower edge at z=1.200 m; the 350–440 mm band stays above the table.
Inspect the knot's fabric path in the video and photographs. It must be a
conventional necktie knot, such as an Oriental or four-in-hand, with the wide
end threaded through its front loop. A loose drape, preformed knot, attached
strip, bow or mere visual overlap does not count. Judge the resulting knot
and fit, not a prescribed arm sequence.

- **0:** no valid necktie knot remains after both grippers release for five
  seconds, or cloth/contact integrity fails.
- **0.5:** a valid knot remains for five seconds but one or more dressing
  conditions below fail.
- **1:** the retained knot is within 20 mm horizontally of the marked collar centreline; the
  neckband's greatest gap from the form is no more than 15 mm; the wide blade hangs flat without an axial half-twist; the narrow end is behind the
  wide end with its tip at least 10 mm above the wide tip; and the wide tip lies
  350–440 mm below the collar's lower edge.

This plain tie is reversible: either face may point outward. The pale and gold
edge marks reveal twists. Check the whole hanging wide blade for an axial
half-twist; a flat tip alone is insufficient.

The five-second hold must finish within the run limit. Pulling the knot apart
after achieving it loses credit. Any torn cloth, visible ribbon passage through itself or a solid, secret grasp
attachment, vertex animation or unsupported tightening invalidates the result.
Unknown integrity must be reviewed before assigning a score. No centreline
topology measure or edge-strain threshold substitutes for the flat-fabric and
visible contact checks.

`scoring.score(record)` calculates credit from these reviewed observations and
requires hashes for the released video and front/rear photographs. The unit
test records are synthetic and cannot be used as task evidence.

The fit bands allow normal variation on this adult collar form. Measure lateral
centring in the front photo, not a three-dimensional distance to the mark on
the neck. The neckband gap is the greatest visible stand-off of the band around
the neck form, excluding the knot and hanging ends. A properly formed knot and
a released hold remain mandatory; the wider length band does not waive them.
